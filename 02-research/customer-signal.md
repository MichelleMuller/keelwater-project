## Sources [INVENTED]

## What we heard, reconfirmed by market research

| Finding | Internal signal | Market research |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed** (what handlers and claimants feel) comes mostly from piles 1 and 2, plus clearing missing information on any claim | **[INVENTED]** Handlers and claims ops leads describe the pain as claims sitting in the queue, not as sorting being hard | Industry triage optimises for cycle time first; non-straight-through claims are mostly adjuster review (30%) and missing info (20%), not fraud (4%) ([research §1–2](motor-claims-triage-and-fraud.md)) |
| **Money and risk** (what insurers' finance and fraud teams feel) sit in pile 3 | **[INVENTED]** Fraud comes up with finance and fraud stakeholders, framed as "don't let speed pay out fraud" rather than "find more fraud" | Fraud is a small share of claims but high value per case: ~£11k per detected UK motor fraud case ([research §2](motor-claims-triage-and-fraud.md)) |
| The 15% of claims taking >4h are more likely **complex or waiting-on-information** claims than fraud | **[INVENTED]** CSMs and AMs point to claims with a third party, injury or missing documents as the ones that get stuck | Third party, injury, disputed liability, total loss and missing documents are the most cited causes of motor claim delays ([research §6](motor-claims-triage-and-fraud.md)) |
