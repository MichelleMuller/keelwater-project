# Submission checklist

## Must answer (the five questions)

- [ ] 1. What problem needs solving, and how confident am I
- [ ] 2. What I need to find out before committing the squad, and how to find it out quickly
- [ ] 3. Prioritisation: first / can wait / won't do, **including bulk sorting**
- [ ] 4. First signal → decision → validation → delivery → measurement
  - [ ] Where AI changes how I work, where it doesn't, what stays my responsibility
  - [ ] How I'd decide automation is **safe and valuable enough** before using it
- [ ] 5. What I tell Sales and CS, and **when**

## End of recording (≤5 min combined)

- [ ] How I used AI: what for, where I overrode it, **one thing it got wrong that I rejected**
- [ ] How the product lifecycle is changing for AI-native teams; what I'd change at Keelwater

## Format and logistics

- [ ] Recording ≤20 minutes
- [ ] Work produced (deck / doc)
- [ ] 1–2 pieces of working material (AI conversation, analysis, prototype, eval, dropped approach)
- [ ] Everything invented is flagged
- [ ] ≤3 hours spent; if cut, say what and why

## Audience

Panel of four: **PM, engineer, designer, commercial**. The deck should give each one something: commercial plan, technical approach and evals, handler UX, product judgement.
